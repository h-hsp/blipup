# BlipUp – image prompts (clean explorer style)

Use these in ChatGPT (or Higgsfield) to redo the 30 original Blips.

## How to use in ChatGPT
1. Make **Blub first**. Paste the **Style block** + Blub's prompt. Ask for a **square image with a transparent background**.
2. Download Blub. That image is your **style reference**.
3. For every other Blip: **upload the Blub image**, then paste:
   `Match the art style, finish, outline and proportions of the attached image exactly, but draw a completely different character.` + the **Style block** + that Blip's prompt.
4. Save each one as its id, e.g. `npc.png`, `lowkey.png` (lowercase, no spaces). That is what the app expects.
5. Rarity lines are already inside each prompt (3★ get a richer glow, 4★ get gold highlights).

## Style block (paste every time)
> A clean, polished, fully finished mobile-game collectible creature called a Blip: a small squishy jelly creature with a smooth glossy translucent body, clean soft gradients and crisp specular highlights, big shiny expressive eyes, tiny stubby arms. It carries a small treasure-explorer item that fits its personality. Smooth digital rendering like official game key art, crisp clean edges. No pencil texture, no brush grain, no sketchy or rough lines, no paper texture. Thick clean white sticker outline around the whole character. Centered, full body, square 1:1, transparent background, no text, no logos.
